Elias Thorne

Chronological Librarian

Elias Thorne is a librarian at the Great Library of Alexandria—at least, he was before it burned down. Now, he travels through time to recover lost manuscripts and ensure that history's most important stories are never forgotten. He is often seen carrying a leather-bound journal and a cup of tea that never seems to go cold.
